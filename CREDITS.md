# Credits

Verdant Isles was designed and playtested by Kristian Weeks, with AI-assisted development using ChatGPT and Codex.

## Environment assets

Much of the scanned scenery, foliage, props, HDR lighting, and ground material comes from **[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/)** under **CC0**.

Prepared models and textures may be simplified, recompressed, recolored, or otherwise adapted for the game. Individual source records are retained in:

- [Model manifest](assets/polyhaven/manifest.json)
- [Asset preparation notes](assets/ASSET-NOTES.txt)
- [Coastal source records](assets/pass14-sources.json)
- [Cabin source records](assets/pass29-sources.json)
- [Ground material records](assets/rough-coastal-source.json)
- [Pine bark records](assets/pine-bark-source.json)

Some preparation notes are historical and describe earlier passes rather than the exact current scene.
