Play a little golf.

40 approved assets · Incorporated in pass 12

A ship at the shore. A few supplies unloaded. A cannon watching the sea. Beyond the cove, a lush forest opens onto a beautifully kept golf course. This shortlist develops that story without filling every space.

Every candidate is a free CC0 Poly Haven asset. These are official source-model previews of the approved collection. Review the next mystical additions here. Your original ten are shown again below and remain approved.

The landing

Barrels, chest and a few supplies form one cargo grouping beside the ship. Place the cannon nearby, with a clear view out to sea and room to walk between props.

The woodland edge

Put the unlit stone fire pit in a sheltered clearing above the sand. Use modest exploration gear rather than tents, beds or signs of a long occupation.

The course

Blend ground cover, flowers and stone groups into the wild margins. Keep the fairway, green, bunkers and walking route clear. Preserve the established light and trees.
