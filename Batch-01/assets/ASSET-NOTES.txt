Verdant Isles / Smuggler's Green / Prototype 10

Environment model sets: Poly Haven, CC0-1.0 (public domain). Official source downloads; selected variants, simplified meshes and recompressed textures. Runtime grass uses the supplied grass_medium_01_mid_b variant.
Evergreen needle fragments widened during preparation to preserve canopy coverage. Pine trunks widened with a taper at load; trees centered on their trunk bases. Jacaranda canopy artistically recolored rose pink.

Prototype 10 ground cover - Poly Haven, CC0-1.0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/moss_01 (moss_01_a_LOD0; 24 triangles)
https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_bermuda_01 (medium_a; 17 triangles)
Separate official alpha masks merged into base-color PNGs; alpha testing prevents dark rectangular borders. The same repair was applied to grass_medium_01 and searsia_lucida. Tree base geometry conforms to terrain locally, with a green vertex-color tint on the outer apron. Shoreline rocks are buried more deeply. Distant islets use original terrain geometry and reused scans.

Prototype 10 terrain surfaces - Poly Haven, CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass
https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
Official 2K diffuse textures and 2K OpenGL normals downloaded; diffuse retained at 2K, normals reduced to 1K and JPEG recompressed. Green tint and world-coordinate blending apply only to wild rough. No displacement modifies golf terrain physics.
