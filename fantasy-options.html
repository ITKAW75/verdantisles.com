11 possibilities for the next pass

Crystals found among old rocks. A fairy glimpsed between trees. Birds above the cove. These additions would bring a quiet sense of magic to the island, with room for the landscape to breathe.

These are sourced model previews for review, not screenshots of new game additions. Select the objects you like and copy your choices back into our chat. Each source offers a free model under CC0 or CC BY 4.0; attribution will accompany any CC BY asset we use.

Hidden discoveries

A crystal cluster or a single old shrine can reward a short walk into the woods. Keep glow subtle and discoveries away from the maintained course.

Small signs of life

Place fairies sparingly among the trees and let birds cross the sky at different heights. Quiet movement should feel natural.

Sea-worn treasures

One bronze sea creature could join the captain’s finds. Relics belong in deliberate groupings, with clear space around them.
